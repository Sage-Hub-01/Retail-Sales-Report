## *Retail Sales Performance Dashboard (Power BI)*

### *Overview*

This Power BI dashboard provides a comprehensive view of retail sales performance. It analyzes key business metrics such as total sales, profit, discount trends, and sales distribution across regions, product categories, and salespeople.

---

### *Key Features*

* *KPI Cards* showing Total Sales, Total Profit, Average Discount, and Total Orders
* *Bar Charts* for:

  * Sales by Product Category
  * Profit by Country
* *Pie Chart* showing regional sales distribution
* *Line Charts* tracking sales and profit over time
* *Salesperson performance* visualized with a bar and scatter chart
* *Interactive slicers* for filtering by:

  * Region
  * Product Category
  * Order Date

---

### *Tools Used*

* Microsoft Power BI Desktop
* Power Query for data transformation
* Basic DAX for calculated measures and formatting

---

### *Dataset*

The dataset includes 500 rows of retail transactions, with the following key fields:

* Order & Ship Dates
* Region and Country
* Product Category and Sub-Category
* Salesperson
* Quantity, Unit Price, Discount
* Sales, Cost, and Profit

*Note*: The original file included currency values as text. These were cleaned and converted to numeric formats in Power BI for accurate reporting.

---

### *How to Use*

1. Download or clone this repository
2. Open Retail_Sales_Dashboard.pbix in *Power BI Desktop*
3. Use the slicers to filter data by Region, Product Category, or Date
4. Explore the visuals for business insights
